## Stance against AI

Generative AI is deeply unethical.

- **They are built on theft**: LLMs are built on stolen data, there is an unfathomable amount of theft that has been done, at the scale of everything.

- **They are destroying history**: Companies like Anthropic have bought en-masse physical copies of books (including very old books) are digitizing them, then destroying them purely for training purposes. See [this forbes article](https://www.forbes.com/sites/maryroeloffs/2026/08/17/ai-companies-are-buying-and-destroying-antique-books-heres-why/) or go read into it yourself.

We can keep going (for a while), but it's immaterial.

DO NOT USE AI in this project. This is a project to make software to be used by actual humans, for activities that humans do, by humans.
